# Archetypes

Starting points, not templates. Pick the closest, then vary. When nothing
fits (e.g. healthcare, public sector), describe a new archetype in
`brief.md` with the same five rows and use it.

## industrial-b2b

Heavy machinery, tools, components, wholesale for trades.

| Axis | Typical choice |
|---|---|
| Colour | Steel/graphite neutrals with a cool or warm cast; one saturated signal colour (orange, yellow, red, safety green, steel blue) as action |
| Contrast | High; dark headings on white, dense information |
| Shape | `0-4px` everywhere |
| Type | Condensed or geometric sans for headings (Barlow Condensed, Oswald, IBM Plex Sans, Archivo); highly legible body with tabular figures (Inter, IBM Plex Sans, Source Sans 3) |
| Voice | Buttons often uppercase with slight tracking |

Variation axes: signal colour temperature, raw vs. refined (condensed vs.
plex), cold steel vs. warm craft.

## luxury-editorial

Fashion, jewellery, premium furniture, wine.

| Axis | Typical choice |
|---|---|
| Colour | Near-monochrome neutrals, one restrained jewel or metallic accent |
| Contrast | Soft body contrast (still AA), strong headline contrast |
| Shape | `0px`, hairline borders |
| Type | Serif display headings (Playfair Display, Cormorant, Libre Caslon); quiet sans or serif body |
| Voice | Sentence case or small caps, generous tracking on labels |

Variation axes: warm ivory vs. cold gallery white, serif vs. high-contrast
sans, accent colour.

## consumer-lifestyle

Home, outdoor, sports, food, DIY retail.

| Axis | Typical choice |
|---|---|
| Colour | Warm neutrals, vivid friendly brand colour, cheerful accent |
| Contrast | Medium-high; colour blocks |
| Shape | `12-24px` containers, pill-ish controls (`8-999px`) |
| Type | Rounded or humanist sans (Nunito, DM Sans, Poppins, Figtree) |
| Voice | Sentence case buttons, no tracking |

Variation axes: playful vs. calm, saturated vs. earthy, round vs. soft-square.

## Making directions differ

Three directions that only change hue are one direction. Change at least two
of: colour temperature, contrast level, corner shape, heading voice, button
voice. Name each direction after the feeling, not the colour ("Raw steel",
"Warm craft", "Clean technical").
